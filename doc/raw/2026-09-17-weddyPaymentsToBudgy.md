# Brief – paid wedding payments copied into IziBudgy

English translation of the project owner's request of 2026-09-17.

---

Great, now we should connect it with Budgy. As soon as I save that a deposit was
paid, or that a payment was paid and it has a price, it should show up in Budgy.
When it changes back to not paid, it should disappear from Budgy again.

---

## Decisions taken with the owner on the same day

1. **Whose budget:** always the **plan admin's**, whoever marks the payment –
   *(the recommended option, "the person who marked it", was not chosen)*.
2. **Category:** a **new Budgy category *Svatba* (Wedding)** – a wedding costs
   hundreds of thousands and in *Other* it would swamp the chart.
3. **Deleting in Weddy** (an item, a bundle or the whole plan) **keeps the
   expense in Budgy as an ordinary entry** – the money has been spent; the entry
   just stops being linked to the plan.
