# Brief – deposits and payment state in IziWeddy planning

English translation of the project owner's request of 2026-09-17.

---

I would also like every section item / bundle in Weddy to have a "Záloha"
(deposit). I would show it with a checkbox. This part will have its own state,
"Uhrazeno" (paid) / "Neuhrazeno" (unpaid). I would also add the state of the
whole item – paid.

---

## Decisions taken with the owner on the same day

1. **The deposit has an amount**, not just a tick and a state – otherwise the
   budget could not say how much is left to pay.
2. **The budget screen shows the payments**: how much has been paid and how
   much is left to pay.
