# Brief – IziBudgy, first version

English translation of the project owner's request of 2026-09-17.

---

Let's start with IziBudgy – a budget application.

It should have Income – there can be several entries.
Expenses – split into sections:
Recurring (mortgage, insurance, phone, internet, streaming platforms, the radio
fee, leasing and so on)
One-off (food, entertainment, household, maybe something else?)

There should be a chart of what and how. Try to come up with something nice.

---

## Decisions taken with the owner on the same day

Asked because the brief leaves the shape of the data open. The older
[izibudgyBrief.md](izibudgyBrief.md) questions (bank import, several
currencies, loans, savings goals) stay unanswered and out of scope.

1. **Time.** A monthly budget **with history**. Recurring entries carry over to
   the following months on their own, one-off entries are entered with a date.
   Months can be browsed backwards and compared.
   *(The alternative – one running budget with no months – was rejected: it
   cannot say how much was spent last month.)*
2. **Sharing.** For now the budget belongs to **one account**; no roles and no
   invitations. Sharing with a partner can be added later the way IziWeddy does
   it, without reshaping the model.
3. **Categories.** A **fixed list**, like the planning sections in IziWeddy:
   housing, insurance, phone and internet, subscriptions, transport, food,
   household, entertainment, health, children, other. Colours in the chart,
   translations and statistics come for free and there is nothing to manage.
